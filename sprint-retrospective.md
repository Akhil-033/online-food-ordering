# Sprint Retrospective

## Sprint 1

### 1. What Went Well?
- Customer Login tasks were completed.
- Restaurant and Menu features were completed.
- The team collaborated to complete the planned tasks.

### 2. What Did Not Go Well?
- Shopping Cart tasks took more time than expected.
- Some tasks needed additional testing.

### 3. What Did We Learn?
- Better effort estimation is needed.
- Large tasks should be divided into smaller tasks.
- Regular communication helps the team.

### 4. Improvements for Sprint 2
- Divide large tasks into smaller sub-issues.
- Estimate story points carefully.
- Test features regularly.
- Discuss blockers during Daily Scrum meetings.

## Conclusion
The retrospective helped the team identify strengths, challenges, and improvements for the next sprint.
