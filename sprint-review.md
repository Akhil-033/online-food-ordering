# Sprint Review

## Sprint 1

### Features Demonstrated
1. Customer Login
2. Browse Restaurants and Menu
3. Shopping Cart

### Demonstration Flow
1. Customer logs in to the application.
2. Customer views available restaurants.
3. Customer selects a restaurant and views its menu.
4. Customer selects food items.
5. Customer adds food items to the cart.
6. Customer views the cart and checks the total amount.

### Sprint Outcome
The Sprint 1 increment covers the planned login, restaurant and menu browsing, and shopping cart features.

### Feedback
The team reviewed the completed tasks and identified improvements for the next sprint.

## Conclusion
The Sprint Review helped the team evaluate Sprint 1 and prepare for Sprint 2.
